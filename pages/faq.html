General Information

What is Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore?

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore is a private Industrial Training Institute located in Sehore, Madhya Pradesh, providing vocational and technical training to students.

Where is the institute located?

The institute is located at Krishan Nagar, Shanti Vihar Housing Board Colony, Sehore - 466001, Madhya Pradesh.

When was the institute established?

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore was established in 2016.

Trades & Courses

Which trades are available at the institute?

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore currently offers Electrician and Health Sanitary Inspector (HSI) trades.

What is the Electrician trade?

The Electrician trade provides vocational training related to electrical systems, installations, maintenance and practical electrical work.

What is the Health Sanitary Inspector trade?

The Health Sanitary Inspector trade focuses on vocational training related to sanitation, hygiene, public health and sanitary practices.
