About Maharana Pratap Private ITI Sehore

MAHARANA PRATAP PRIVATE ITI SEHORE is a private Industrial Training Institute located in Sehore, Madhya Pradesh. The institute was established in the year 2016 with the objective of providing quality vocational education and practical skill training to trainees.

The institute focuses on developing technical knowledge, practical skills and professional abilities among trainees. Through practical training and workshop-based learning, the institute aims to prepare trainees for employment and future career opportunities.

The institute provides vocational training in Electrician and Health Sanitary Inspector trades and emphasizes practical learning as an important part of technical education.
