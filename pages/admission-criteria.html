Required Documents for ITI Admission

All candidates taking admission in the current session are hereby informed to bring the following documents mandatorily to complete the admission process —

Required Documents

  1. 10th Marksheet
  2. 12th Marksheet / Graduation or Other Marksheet (If Available)
  3. Aadhaar Card
  4. Samagra ID
  5. Passport Size Photographs (4–8)
  6. Domicile Certificate
  7. Caste Certificate (For SC/ST/OBC Candidates)
  8. Income Certificate
  9. Transfer Certificate (TC)
  10. Migration Certificate (If from Other Board)
  11. Bank Passbook Copy
  12. Mobile Number & Email ID

Required for Scholarship

  1. APAAR / ABC ID
  2. OTR Registration
  3. MPTAAS Profile
Note: Original documents along with two sets of photocopies are mandatory.
