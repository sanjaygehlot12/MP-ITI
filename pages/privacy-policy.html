Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore collects, uses and protects information provided through this website.

Last Updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore ("the Institute", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of people who visit this website, submit an enquiry, apply for admission or make an admission-related payment. This Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, how it may be shared and how you can contact us about it.

This Policy applies to information collected through this website and through the enquiry, admission, document-upload and payment-verification processes described here. It should be read together with any notices shown at the point where information is collected.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect the following information that you provide:

  • Enquiry information: name, phone number, email address and the message or course details you provide.
  • Admission information: applicant and parent/guardian names, date of birth, contact details, residential address, educational qualification, selected trade and other details requested in the admission form.
  • Uploaded documents: photographs and documents submitted to support an admission application, such as identity, education or other records requested in the form.
  • Payment-verification information: payment method selected, UTR or transaction reference number, payment screenshot and details needed by the Institute to match and verify a fee payment. Payment is made using the UPI QR/payment instructions or bank-transfer details provided by the Institute.
  • Technical and security information: information that may be provided by your browser or collected by website security services, such as IP address, browser/device information and CAPTCHA/Turnstile verification data.

Please provide only information requested for the relevant enquiry, admission or payment-verification purpose. Do not upload unrelated or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information for Institute-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and providing information about courses, eligibility and admissions.
  • Receiving, reviewing and processing admission applications.
  • Checking applicant details and documents and contacting applicants about missing or unclear information.
  • Recording and verifying admission-fee payments using the UTR/reference number and payment screenshot you submit.
  • Maintaining student, academic, administrative, accounting and fee records.
  • Sending important admission, course, training or Institute-related communications.
  • Protecting the website, detecting misuse and reducing automated or fraudulent submissions.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, affiliation and government-record requirements.

4. Payments and Payment Verification

The Institute may provide payment instructions through a UPI QR code and/or bank transfer. After making a payment, applicants may be asked to submit the UTR/transaction reference number and a screenshot or receipt so that the Institute can identify and verify the payment and update its fee records.

UPI payments are completed through the applicant's UPI app or bank, and bank transfers are processed by the relevant banks. The Institute does not ask applicants to provide their UPI PIN, internet-banking password, card PIN, CVV or one-time password (OTP) through this website or through an uploaded screenshot. Never share these credentials with anyone.

Payment verification information may be accessed by authorised Institute personnel for reconciliation, accounting, admission and dispute-resolution purposes. Applicants should check the payment details carefully before transferring money and contact the Institute if a payment is debited but not reflected in the Institute's records.

5. Documents and Student Records

Documents uploaded with an admission application are used to review and process that application and, where admission proceeds, to maintain relevant student and institutional records. Access should be limited to authorised personnel who need the information for their Institute duties.

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that the information and documents they submit are accurate, complete and relate to the applicant. Where an applicant is a minor, a parent or lawful guardian should be involved and provide any consent or information required under applicable law.

6. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • To authorised Institute staff or management involved in enquiries, admissions, document review, fee verification and record keeping.
  • To technology providers that support website hosting, database/storage or website security. The website uses Supabase for certain database and document-storage functions and Cloudflare Turnstile for automated-submission protection.
  • To banks, payment or UPI service providers as part of the payment transaction, which are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.
  • To relevant government, affiliation, examination or regulatory authorities when required for admission, training, student records or compliance.
  • To a competent authority where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Service providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services. Their own privacy notices and terms may also apply to their services.

7. Website Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information, which may include restricted access to admission records and uploaded documents, website security checks and access controls for authorised users.

No website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use a secure device and network when submitting information, and contact us promptly if you believe information submitted to the Institute has been misused.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where needed for website operation, security or performance. Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical information to help distinguish legitimate visitors from automated traffic.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or scripts may affect certain website features or form submissions.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may link to government portals, social-media platforms, maps, banks, UPI apps or other external services. When you visit or use those services, their own privacy policies, terms and security practices apply. The Institute does not control third-party websites and is not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

10. Data Retention

We retain enquiry, admission, document and payment-verification information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, processing admissions, maintaining student and financial records, meeting applicable affiliation or legal requirements and resolving disputes.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable record-retention obligations and any lawful need to preserve it. The exact retention period may vary by record type and applicable requirements.

11. Your Privacy Requests and Grievances

You may contact the Institute to request information about personal data you have submitted, ask for correction or updating of inaccurate information, or raise a concern about how your information is handled. Requests will be considered and handled in accordance with applicable law and any record-keeping obligations that apply to the Institute.

To help us locate the relevant record, include your name and the contact details used in the enquiry or admission application. Please do not send passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs or other account credentials. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you may use the grievance or complaint mechanism available under applicable Indian law, where applicable.

12. Children's Privacy

Some applicants may be below 18 years of age. Where an applicant is a child under applicable law, the required parent or lawful-guardian involvement and verifiable consent must be obtained before personal data is processed, as applicable. A parent or lawful guardian may contact the Institute using the details below with a question or request concerning a child's information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy when our website, admission or payment-verification processes change, or when applicable legal requirements change. The revised Policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Please review this page periodically.

14. Contact Us

For questions, privacy requests or grievances relating to this Policy or the handling of information submitted through this website, contact the Institute using the details below.
