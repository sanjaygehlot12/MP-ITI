Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore handles personal information collected through this website, admission enquiries, admission applications and related Institute services.

Last Updated: 11 November 2026

1. About This Policy

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore ("Institute", "we", "us" or "our") is responsible for the personal information described in this Policy. This Policy applies when you browse our website, contact us, submit an enquiry, apply for admission, provide student or parent/guardian details, or otherwise communicate with us through the channels described here. The website currently does not provide document-upload or online payment/fee-verification facilities.

This Policy should be read with any short privacy or consent notice displayed beside a form or at the point where information is collected. If a specific form explains an additional use of information, that notice should also be read before submitting the form.

We aim to collect only information reasonably required for the stated Institute purpose and to handle it in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules, to the extent and from the dates those provisions apply.

2. Personal Information We May Collect

The information collected depends on the service or form you use. It may include:

  • Enquiry details: name, mobile number, email address, course or trade of interest and the message you send us.
  • Applicant and student details: applicant's full name, date of birth, contact details, residential address, educational qualification, selected trade, admission/session details and information needed to maintain academic or administrative records.
  • Parent or lawful-guardian details: where an applicant is below 18 years of age, or where otherwise required, the parent/guardian's name, relationship to the applicant, mobile number, email address if provided, and information reasonably needed to contact and verify the parent/guardian and record consent.
  • Communications: messages, calls or emails exchanged with the Institute, including follow-up information you choose to provide.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser/device information, basic usage or diagnostic logs and CAPTCHA/Turnstile verification signals that may be processed to secure forms and prevent spam or misuse.

Please submit only accurate information requested by the relevant form. The website currently does not ask you to upload admission documents or submit payment details through an online payment facility.

3. How We Receive Information

We generally receive information directly from the applicant, parent or lawful guardian through website forms, email, telephone, WhatsApp or in-person communication. We may also receive technical or security information from hosting, database or website-security providers when needed to operate and protect the website.

4. Why We Use Personal Information

We use personal information only for relevant and specified Institute purposes, which may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and explaining courses, eligibility, fees and admission procedures.
  • Receiving, assessing and processing admission applications and contacting applicants about missing, incorrect or unclear details.
  • Contacting a parent or lawful guardian and obtaining or recording their involvement or consent where required for an applicant below 18 years of age.
  • Maintaining admission, trainee, academic, examination, attendance, training, placement, affiliation and institutional records, as applicable.
  • Sending important admission, training, examination, fee, safety or Institute-related notices.
  • Operating, maintaining and securing the website; preventing spam, impersonation, fraud and unauthorised access; and troubleshooting technical problems.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, government, examination, affiliation and record-keeping requirements, and responding to lawful requests.
  • Handling complaints, requests, disputes and safeguarding concerns.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use student information for targeted advertising or behavioural advertising.

5. Consent, Notices and Your Choices

Where consent is the basis for processing, we will provide a notice describing the categories of information and the purpose for which it is requested, and seek an affirmative action such as selecting an unchecked consent box or another appropriate method. A person should not submit an application on the assumption that merely visiting this page gives consent.

Where consent is given, it may be withdrawn by contacting the Institute using the details in Section 15. Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful and may affect our ability to continue an enquiry or service that depends on the information. We may continue to retain or process information where permitted or required by applicable law, affiliation rules, accounting requirements or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where information is processed for a legal obligation or another permitted purpose, we will handle it within the scope of that purpose and applicable law. We will not treat a consent checkbox as permission for unrelated uses.

6. Applicants Below 18 Years of Age

The Institute accepts admission applications from applicants who may be below 18 years of age. For an applicant below 18, the applicant's parent or lawful guardian must be involved in the admission process. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain and record the parent or lawful guardian's consent before processing the child's personal information on the basis of that consent.

For this purpose, we may collect the parent/guardian's name, relationship to the applicant, mobile number and email address (if available), and a record of the consent provided, including the date and time, the relevant notice/policy version and the application reference. We may contact the parent/guardian and take reasonable steps to verify that the person is an adult and is authorised to act as the applicant's parent or lawful guardian. Verification may include an OTP or other appropriate confirmation method. We will not ask for an Aadhaar OTP, UPI PIN, banking password or similar secret credential.

The applicant's own acknowledgement of the form is not a substitute for parent/guardian consent where such consent is legally required. If the parent/guardian does not provide the required consent, the Institute may be unable to process the online application until the required steps are completed.

Information about a minor will be used for admission, education, training, safety, administration and other lawful Institute purposes. We do not knowingly use children's personal information for targeted advertising or behavioural monitoring.

A parent or lawful guardian may contact us to ask questions or make a request concerning the child's information, subject to reasonable verification and applicable law.

7. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only to the extent reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • Institute personnel: authorised staff and management responsible for enquiries, admissions, student services, safety and record keeping.
  • Technology and service providers: providers supporting website hosting, database, security, communications or technical operations. The website uses Supabase for certain database/storage functions and Cloudflare Turnstile for automated-submission protection.
  • Government, examination and affiliation authorities: where information is required for admission, training, examination, certification, affiliation, scholarship or other official processes.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law, court order or valid legal process.

Where a service provider processes information on our behalf, it should do so for the service we engage it to provide and subject to appropriate contractual or other safeguards. External services may also have their own privacy notices and terms.

8. Storage, Security and Data Breaches

We use reasonable administrative, organisational and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse. Depending on the system, safeguards may include access restrictions, authentication, security checks, backups and limiting access to personnel who need the information for Institute work.

No website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a secure device and network and avoid sending passwords, OTPs or financial credentials.

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting information handled by the Institute, we will assess and respond to it and provide notices or reports to affected individuals and relevant authorities as required by applicable law. Please contact us promptly if you suspect that information submitted to the Institute has been misused.

7. Cookies and Website Security Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies required for basic operation, security, preferences or performance. Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical and browser signals to help distinguish legitimate form submissions from automated or abusive traffic.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies or scripts may affect website features or form submissions. We do not use these technologies to create advertising profiles of students.

8. Third-Party Websites and External Services

This website may link to government portals, DGT/NCVT services, scholarship portals, social-media platforms, maps or other external websites. When you visit or use those services, their own privacy policies, terms and security practices apply. The Institute does not control their independent processing of information.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, processing admission, maintaining student and institutional records, meeting applicable legal, government, examination and affiliation requirements, and handling disputes or safeguarding matters. This website currently does not collect uploaded admission documents or online payment details.

Different records may need to be kept for different periods. When information is no longer required and no legal or institutional retention requirement applies, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted in the ordinary backup cycle.

8. Your Requests and Grievances

Subject to applicable law and verification, you may contact the Institute to request information about personal data you have provided, ask for correction or updating of inaccurate information, request erasure where permitted, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a grievance about how information is handled.

To help us locate the relevant record, include your name, application/reference number if available, and the mobile number or email used in the application. Do not include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or other account credentials. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity or, for a minor applicant, your authority as parent or lawful guardian.

We will acknowledge and handle requests within the period required by applicable law and our grievance process. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may pursue any complaint or remedy available under applicable Indian law.
