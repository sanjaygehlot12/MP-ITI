Principal's Profile

SANDEEP MALVIYA

Principal

The Principal provides academic and administrative leadership to the institute and works towards maintaining a disciplined, skill-oriented and supportive learning environment for trainees.

The institute focuses on quality vocational training, practical learning and the overall development of trainees to help them build relevant skills for employment and future career opportunities.

Principal's Message

Welcome to MAHARANA PRATAP PRIVATE ITI SEHORE.

Our aim is to provide trainees with quality vocational education along with strong practical exposure so that they can develop the technical skills, confidence and discipline required for the world of work.

At our institute, we encourage trainees to learn through practical training, develop a responsible attitude and continuously improve their knowledge and skills.

I encourage every trainee to make the best use of the training opportunities available at the institute and work sincerely towards building a successful future.

— SANDEEP MALVIYA
Principal

Management's Role & Responsibilities

  • Maintaining an effective and disciplined training environment for trainees.
  • Supporting quality practical and theoretical vocational training.
  • Ensuring proper functioning and maintenance of training facilities and workshops.
  • Encouraging skill development, discipline and professional behaviour among trainees.
  • Supporting industry interaction and employment-oriented training opportunities.
  • Promoting the overall academic and professional development of trainees.

Institute Leadership

The management and institute leadership are committed to providing a professional learning environment where trainees can develop practical skills and prepare themselves for employment and future opportunities.

For institute-related enquiries, trainees and parents may contact the institute through the official contact details provided on this website.
