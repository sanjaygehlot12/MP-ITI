Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about admission, trades, eligibility and training.

How can I apply for admission?

Students can apply for admission by following the admission process provided by Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore. For detailed information about eligibility, required documents and the admission procedure, please visit the Admission section of this website or contact the institute.

Which trades are available at the institute?

Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore currently offers Electrician and Health Sanitary Inspector (HSI) trades. Students can visit the Trades section for detailed information about the available courses.

What is the eligibility for ITI admission?

Eligibility requirements may vary according to the trade and applicable admission guidelines. Students are advised to check the latest admission information before applying.

What documents are required for admission?

Students may be required to provide educational certificates, identity proof, photographs and other documents as applicable during the admission process.

Is scholarship available for ITI students?

Eligible students may apply for scholarships according to applicable government scholarship schemes and their respective eligibility criteria.

How can I contact the institute?

You can contact Maharana Pratap Pvt ITI Sehore through the phone number, email address or WhatsApp contact provided on this website. You can also visit the Contact page for complete contact information.
